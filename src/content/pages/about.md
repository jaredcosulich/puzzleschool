### Where this comes from

Adults spend their working lives on questions nobody has framed yet: what a
community needs, what a job actually is, what to do when the instructions run
out. Almost none of that resembles the tasks children are given to practise on.

The Puzzle School starts from the other end. Every day opens with something
unresolved, and the ordinary subjects are taught in the course of working on it.

### What we mean by a puzzle

Not a jigsaw, and not a riddle with a trick. A puzzle here is any situation
where the shape of the answer is unknown and progress has to be made anyway,
with enough structure around it that a ten-year-old can get purchase.

Some are mathematical. Most are not: how to divide something that will not
divide fairly, why a rule nobody can explain is still followed, what a
neighbourhood is missing.
