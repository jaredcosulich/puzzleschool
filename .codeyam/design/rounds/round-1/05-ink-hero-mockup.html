The Puzzle School

An experimental K–12 school

A K-12 educational experience focused on ambiguity.

Ambiguity is everywhere in our lives, especially in learning, but also in who we are, what we care about, and how we live with, treat, and collaborate with each other. Maybe more importantly ambiguity exists in our communities and society, how we can add value to them, how they are structured, and how we can go about influencing that.

Puzzles represent structured exercises in navigating ambiguity.
